This project was part of a longitudinaly study funded by the <a href="https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/9319426">NIH</a> (award no. 2R01DC011742, P.I. Karla McGregor). Here we compare age-related changes in nonword repetition accuracy from 7 to 10 years of age for children with and without Developmental Language Disorder (DLD).

<h2> SRCLD 2026 </h2>

We presented this research at the 2026 <a href="https://srcld.wisc.edu">Symposium on Research in Child Language Disorders</a> as a <a href="https://srcld.wisc.edu/submitted-oral-presentations/">Submitted Oral Presentation</a>.
